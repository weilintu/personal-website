About

Research Overview

I am a theoretical condensed matter physicist specializing in tensor network methods and their application to quantum many-body systems. My research centers on tensor network ansätze such as matrix product states (MPS) and projected entangled-pair states (PEPS).

These computational tools allow me to study a wide range of quantum phenomena including quantum magnetism, cold-atomic systems, correlated fermionic materials, and high-temperature superconductors.

I am currently a Project Lecturer at Keio University, Japan, following research positions at Korea University and the Institute for Solid State Physics (ISSP) at the University of Tokyo.
