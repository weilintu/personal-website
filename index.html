Theoretical Condensed Matter Physics

Wei-Lin Tu

Project Lecturer — Faculty of Science and Technology, Keio University

My research centers on tensor network ansätze—matrix product states (MPS) and projected entangled-pair states (PEPS)—as powerful computational frameworks for understanding quantum many-body systems, quantum magnetism, cold-atomic systems, and correlated fermionic materials, including high-temperature superconductors.

About

Research Overview

I am a theoretical condensed matter physicist with a focus on developing and applying tensor network methods to strongly correlated quantum systems. My work spans from foundational algorithmic advances—such as variational tensor network operators and generating functions for diagrammatic summation—to physical applications including quantum phase transitions, supersolid phases, Bose-Einstein condensation, and the interplay of orders in cuprate superconductors.

I have held research positions across Japan, South Korea, France, and Taiwan, and I am currently a Project Lecturer at Keio University. My work has been published in leading journals including PRX Quantum, Physical Review Research, Communications Physics, and Scientific Reports.
